# Mesés önuralom / Taleful — concept

*The first children's story channel that knowingly ends.*

> This is an English working version of the concept. The canonical, more detailed Hungarian
> concept document lives as a Claude Doc (linked from the project knowledge base). The two
> websites (`site-hu`, `site-en`) also carry the concept in full, in both languages.

## The problem

For families with little children (roughly ages 0–3), everyday screen time has three recurring pains:

- **"What's right for them now?"** — it's hard to decide quickly which story suits a child at a given age and moment.
- **The ending is always a battle** — before a bath or bedtime, switching the story off means tears and bargaining.
- **It never ends** — most children's content is endless; autoplay always offers "just one more".

## The solution — a story with an order to it

Five simple steps that give the **decision to the parent** and the **ending to the child**:

1. **Who's watching now?** — the parent picks the child (or "everyone", if several watch together); the system knows their age.
2. **Heart or mind?** — the parent answers a question they can actually answer: is it empathy or thinking that's needed now?
3. **Age-appropriate story and length** — the system offers the right story at a length that fits the age.
4. **Getting ready and counting down** — the child learns how long it lasts; a visible, dwindling indicator (stars) counts down.
5. **The child closes it** — at the end, the child closes the story themselves, led in by a calming goodnight ritual, and earns a small, calming bit of praise for stopping.

The ending builds a bridge to the next thing (bath, lunch, leaving) — **no "next episode", no autoplay.** It is not only a bedtime product: it works before lunch or before heading out too, wherever a child needs to stop. Each situation can get its own ending later.

## The classification (what makes it trustworthy)

Human experts — not an algorithm — classify each story. The core is two axes:

- **Heart** (empathy, emotions, connection) — scored 0–3.
- **Mind** (thinking, discovery, problem-solving) — scored 0–3.

Plus **age bands** (0–18 months … 4–5 years), **length**, **stickiness** (how strongly it grabs attention), and a **scary-content** flag. Every story is scored by **at least two experts, independently**, grounded in developmental psychology. See `pontozo/` for the scoring template.

## The switch-off experience

The story doesn't simply stop — it *prepares* the child. Storybook **stars count down** (little ones don't yet grasp minutes), "falling asleep" one by one. The same short goodbye comes every time. The child presses a big button (a TV button, or on screen on a tablet) to close it. The battle becomes a familiar, safe ritual, and the child owns the decision: *"it's happening to me"* becomes *"I'm doing it."*

## The five roles (open positions)

- **The pirate — content:** finding, selecting, trimming to length and organising the 0–3 story library.
- **The researcher — classification:** the heart/mind system, age bands, methodology, the independent expert workflow.
- **The developer — system:** a turnkey, open-source ISO, control software, smart-home integration, the mobile/tablet app.
- **The lawyer — legal:** licensing, copyright, children's data (GDPR), the conditions for a proper launch.
- **The designer — look:** the control software's look, the selectable themes, usability.
- **(The sixth — what we don't yet know.)**

## Two paths — one way or another, it has to exist

1. **On our own, at home** — a parent builds it for their own children: slowly, from whatever is to hand. It can work for one family, but sourcing the stories lawfully and making them widely, legally available stays unsolved.
2. **Championed** — a serious partner (for example public-service media) licenses the stories and provides a team and resources, so it becomes a real, legal product for every family.

Both paths share the same principles: **open communication and published results.** A grassroots start can be the entry point and proof for the championed path.

> *"I see both paths as viable, and one doesn't rule out the other — learning from each other is no great effort. For wide distribution, though, a clear conscience, confident teammates and an orderly setting wouldn't go amiss either."* — a father

## Boundary

Publicly distributing copyrighted content is **not** part of this open, legal project. The legal route for content is **licensing**. This repository contains only original work (concept, documentation, design, code) and an empty methodology template.
