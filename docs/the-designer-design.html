The designer — the visual design

Design decides whether the programme is friendly, trustworthy and easy to use. Here is everything you need to dream it further.

The role

The visual design is not decoration but the carrier of trust and usability — above all the look of the control software, which children and parents see day after day.

Theme system

The look is selectable. The base is the clean icon set; the Matyó folk-tale look is an option. The logic stays the same; only the icons and colours change.

Clean base default
Star, heart, mind — clean icons, good contrast, maximum usability.
Matyó folk-tale option
Folk flowers, warm linen — for the website and the documents, and as an option in the programme.

The indicator defaults to the dwindling star; the 'opening-and-closing' Matyó rose is a lovely option.

Usability-first (0–3)

Large targets

Easy for even a 2-year-old to press.

Little text

Pictures and sound for non-readers.

Calm animations

Slow, winding-down motion — no flashing.

Night mode

A quiet, warm, dark palette for bedtime.

Nothing that leads away

No advertising, 'next episode' or external links.

Accessibility

Contrast, size and clarity for child and parent.
