# Taleful — the short pitch

**The first children's story channel that knowingly ends.**

Every parent of a toddler knows the three screen-time pains: *what's right for them now?*, *switching it off is a battle*, and *it never ends*. Taleful fixes all three.

- The **parent answers one question they can actually answer — heart or mind?** — instead of guessing genres.
- The system serves an **age-appropriate story at a sensible length.**
- A visible countdown (stars that "fall asleep") prepares the child, and **the child closes the story themselves** with a big button — turning the nightly battle into a calm ritual.
- **No autoplay, no "next episode".** The ending bridges to bath, lunch or leaving.

Not an algorithm but **human, developmental-psychology-based classification** (heart/mind, age bands, length, stickiness), scored by at least two experts.

**Why it's different:** a conscious end, the parent decides, the child closes it, human expertise, age-first, good for parent *and* child.

**How it can happen:** it needs no vast apparatus. A small, committed team — ideally within **public-service media**, which already has the resources, experience and research ties — could build a streaming app that serves families and fits the public-service mission.
