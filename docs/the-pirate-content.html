The pirate — content

Building the story library: finding, selecting, trimming to length and organising the important stories of the 0–3 canon.

Tasks

The content branch builds the library in four steps. (Classification belongs to the researcher, the technical system to the developer.)

Finding

Which stories matter at ages 0–3.

Selecting

By age band and heart/mind.

Trimming to length

Short episodes, not hour-long bundles.

Organising

Clean folders and file names.

Quality criteria

Good quality

Clear picture and sound.

One episode = one file

No more than one story per file.

Consistent names

Series, season, episode — uniformly.

An important boundary

The open project covers the system, the classification and the library's curation (finding, selecting, trimming, organising). Sourcing and using the content is the user's own decision and responsibility. Publicly distributing copyrighted content is not part of the open, legal project; the legal route is licensing — see the developer branch and a proper launch.

Milestone

The 0–3 story library comes together: the important stories, in good quality, trimmed to a good length, organised — ready for classification and slotting into the system.
